If you work in risk management in the government, you'll find that compared to businesses, the risks faced by governments are simply too many—so many that they are practically unmanageable.

But to be honest, there are indeed many risks that are completely unnecessary. Some minor issues that wouldn't even be considered risks in any business can waste a lot of resources in the government.

I don't know what the solution to this is. All we can do is try to strike a balance. For governments, risk management leans far more toward AVOIDANCE rather than ACCEPTANCE, even if the probability is very low.
